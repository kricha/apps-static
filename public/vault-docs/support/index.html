02

Common questions

Where are my documents stored?

Documents remain on your device by default. If you enable iCloud Sync, encrypted private copies are stored in your personal iCloud database.

Do I need an account?

Vault Docs does not require a separate proprietary account. Optional sync uses the iCloud account already configured on your Apple device.

How do I add documents?

You can import a PDF, image, photo, or text file, or scan several paper pages into one PDF with the camera. After a scan or import, review the suggested document type, title, and category, then edit anything you want before saving.

How does Smart Processing handle my document?

Text recognition and document classification run locally on your device. Document images, recognized text, and organization suggestions are not sent to kRicha or an external AI provider.

How do I enable iCloud Sync?

Open Vault Docs Settings and enable iCloud Sync. Your device must be signed in to iCloud. Document contents and metadata are encrypted before private copies are uploaded.

What should I do if iCloud Sync is not working?

Check your internet connection, iCloud sign-in, and that iCloud Drive is enabled. Reopen Vault Docs and try Sync Now. If the problem continues, contact support and include the exact error message.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and select Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I export or delete my data?

You can export stored data or use Clear All Data in Settings. Turning iCloud Sync off alone does not delete copies already stored in iCloud.

03

Reporting a problem

To help diagnose an issue, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your Vault Docs version
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Steps that reproduce the problem
  • The exact error message, if one appears

Never include your passcode or private documents in a support message.
