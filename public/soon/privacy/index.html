Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

This policy explains how Soon handles information. Soon is operated independently under the name kRicha.

Policy contents

Overview App data Notifications Data collection Retention & deletion Children Policy changes Contact

01

Overview

Soon is a local-first household supply tracker and shopping-list utility for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

  • Your app data stays on your device.
  • Soon has no account, backend, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • kRicha does not collect or sell personal data through Soon.

02

App data

Soon stores supply names, categories, purchase dates, typical durations, shopping lead times, forecasts, status changes, cycle history, and shopping-list entries locally on your device.

This information is used only on the device to calculate approximate run-out dates, update the Buy list, and show your supply history.

03

Notifications

If you grant permission, Soon schedules local notifications on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. Soon does not use a push-notification server.

04

Data collection and sharing

Soon does not transmit app data to kRicha or third parties. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, account, payment, or cloud-sync service.

Soon does not sell or share personal data. It does not request your contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

05

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete individual supplies or uninstall Soon. Apple device backups may preserve app data according to your device and iCloud backup settings; those backups are controlled by Apple.

Because Soon has no proprietary account or backend, kRicha cannot access or remotely delete data stored on your device or in your Apple-managed backup.

06

Children

Soon is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

07

Policy changes

This policy may change if Soon’s features or legal requirements change. Material updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
